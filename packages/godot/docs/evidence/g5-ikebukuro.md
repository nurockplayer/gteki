## How the walk numbers are made

- **Steering**: the walker walks the route polyline itself: it turns toward the next vertex only once it is
  within 0.05 m of the current one, so every vertex is cut by less than 0.05 m in plan and the walk never takes a
  chord across a corner that the line does not take. The same rule is used on every route.
- **Walked length**: summed 3D distance the walker's feet travel while following the route polyline waypoint
  by waypoint. Every vertex is tied from inside the vertex reach, so it can add or cut less than 0.05 m of path,
  and the ground height the walk follows is damped (~0.2 m lag on a stair); the error is bounded per vertex, not
  proportionally to the route. The tolerance is therefore `max(0.5 m, 0.05 m x polyline vertices)`: the measured
  value is in the table and a route outside the band is a finding, not tolerance noise.
- **Stopped**: a leg that makes no horizontal progress for 60 steps (1 s) or that exceeds twice its
  straight-line walking time plus 600 steps. No route may stop: a stopped route fails the test.
- **Body-block episodes**: runs of steps the walker's own body test (`bodyBlock`: radius 0.3 m, rays at 0.5 / 1.2 m)
  refused. Slide moves along the surface the walker hit are not blocks. No route may have one; a failure names
  the collider that refused the step, the walker's feet where it stood and the clearance to the surface's plane.
- **Floor changes**: every sample of the walker's own height is put in the band of the station floor whose
  elevation is nearest (`floorChanges` counts graph floor transitions, so this is an independent observation
  of the same thing, not a copy of the route). Height damping lags a stair by ~0.2 m, far inside half of the
  4.6 m separation between this station's floors.
- **Walk time**: steps at the web's walk speed 1.45 m/s (60 Hz fixed step); reported, not asserted.
