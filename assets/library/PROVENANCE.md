# GTEki kit library — provenance and rights

Status: kit `0.1.0`, contract `gteki-asset/0.1`. Covers every file under `assets/library/`, including the loose finish textures under `assets/library/finishes/` and `assets/library/mobile/finishes/`.

## Summary

| Input class | Source | Third-party rights involved | Redistribution in public repo / Web bundle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geometry (all GLBs) | Generated by code in `tools/blender/gteki_assets/` from `tools/blender/catalog/*.json` parameters | None — no scans, traces, purchased or downloaded models | Allowed by the project (original work) |
| Procedural textures (terrazzo, tiles, metals, tactile blocks, treads, ceiling spandrels, sign faces, …) | Seeded numpy synthesis in `texsynth.py` and family modules; embedded in GLBs, and, for the finish pack, also shipped as loose `finishes/**/*.jpg` maps (the same kit output, see below) | None — no photographs, no third-party texture packs | Allowed (original work) |
| Poster artwork `source/posters/poster_0[1-8].jpg` | Generated 2026-10-20 with OpenAI image generation through Codex (`image_gen`), exact prompts and SHA-256 in `source/posters/provenance.json` | Governed by the image provider's terms (Owner to confirm before licensing); prompts forbid text, logos, brands, real people and real places; each image was visually screened (no glyphs, marks or identifiable places seen) | Kept in the repo pending the Owner's licence decision; keep the provenance file with the images. Not reproducible bit-for-bit — the JPEGs themselves are the source of record |

**Not used anywhere:** operator station maps or diagrams, operator photographs, Wikimedia or other photographs, PLATEAU / GIS geometry, brand marks, operator typefaces, JIS/ISO pictogram artwork, commercial game assets, Poly Haven or other downloaded materials. Sign faces contain only colour fields and simple geometric arrows; real wording is supplied at runtime by station leads.

## Finish pack textures

The finish pack (`assets/library/finishes/`, and the same under `mobile/`; contract §9) ships the kit's procedural materials a second time as loose `albedo.jpg`, `orm.jpg` and `normal.jpg` files plus a `finish.json` each, next to flat sample GLBs that embed the same JPEG bytes. The loose files are procedural kit output exactly like the textures embedded in the GLBs: seeded numpy synthesis from `texsynth.py` with the kit's seeds, no photographs, no third-party texture packs, no downloaded materials, and no AI-generated images. The mobile pack uses the same generators at half the density. The rights statement for procedural textures above applies to them unchanged.

**Not before #131 merges.** Until PR #131 (the licensing change) has merged and the pack is rebuilt under it, the pack has no licence of its own: `finish.json` copies `contract.LICENSE` (`UNDECIDED`), as the sample GLBs do, and the loose JPEGs are not covered by any SPDX or REUSE annotation. A station should not ship or publish them before that. The licence string in `finish.json` switches with the `EMBED_SPDX` rebuild of the library.

## Dimensions

Geometry uses either `generic-typical` dimensions (widely published typical/standard values: e.g. riser ≤ 160 mm / tread 300 mm stairs, 30° escalators with S1000/S600 step widths, ~0.6 m standard and ~0.9 m wide fare-gate aisles, 300 mm tactile blocks with the JIS T 9251 dot/bar layout family) or `synthetic` look-dev values. Every GLB records its basis in `extras.gteki.dimension_basis` and every parameter in `extras.gteki.params`. **No asset is a measurement of a specific station.** A station lead who rebuilds a variant with sourced measurements must record that evidence in their own station lane before labelling it `station-verified`.

## Licence

The repository licence is still undecided by the Owner (see README). Until then every GLB carries `license: "UNDECIDED — original GTEki work; repository licence pending Owner decision"`.

What the record supports, and what it does not:

- **Geometry and procedural textures** are produced entirely by the code in this repository from catalog parameters; no external file is read. Choosing a licence for them is the Owner's decision alone.
- **Poster images** are AI-generated. The record is limited to the prompts, generation date, SHA-256 hashes (`source/posters/provenance.json`) and our visual screening (no text, logos, marks or identifiable places were seen). That screening is not a legal clearance: before choosing a licence, or before any commercial release, the Owner should confirm the image provider's current terms, and should decide whether AI-generated art is acceptable for that release. Swapping the posters is cheap: `poster_lightbox` takes any repo-relative `poster_image` path, and the GLBs rebuild with `build.sh`. This record covers only the kit's own posters: a supplied `poster_image` must come with `poster_provenance` and `poster_license` params, which replace the kit's claim in that GLB's `extras.gteki` and its manifest entry.

## Regeneration

`tools/blender/build.sh` rebuilds all GLBs and `manifest.json` from the catalog; `tools/blender/build.sh repro` checks two clean builds are byte-identical.

`assets/library/mobile/` is the same catalog built with `PROFILE=mobile tools/blender/build.sh` (`PROFILE=mobile … repro` checks it). It comes from the same inputs as the full library and adds none. Its palette texture holds averages of the same procedural textures; its sign faces and posters are re-encoded copies of the same images. The rights above apply unchanged.
