## How to read the statuses

| Status | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| PASS | Concrete evidence is cited, on a SHA that is the one being accepted (or the difference is stated). |
| GAP | Evidence is missing, stale, or only partly covers the criterion. Unknown stays GAP. |
| HOLD | Blocked by a decision or fact that only the Owner or an outside party can provide. |
| FAIL | Evidence shows the criterion is not met. |
| "published under override" | The Owner's override at the #16 merge is the only basis. It is never a rights PASS. |

"Lane B alone?" means the item can be closed by changes under `stations/shinjuku/**` and `docs/stations/shinjuku/**` plus Lane B's own runs, with no change in another lane's files and no outside answer.
