# Shinjuku network expansion survey — 2026-10-21

Research worker W5; English text with Japanese source names. This is an evidence and acquisition report, **not a release approval or a new routable station graph**. The existing accepted scope remains the E27 candidate described in [the lane README](../README.md), [source register](../../../research/source-register.md), and [research dossier](../../../research/GTEki_Public_Data_Research_2026-10-20.md). No app code or existing authority was changed.

## Findings that change the expansion decision

1. **東京都「新宿ターミナル屋内地図及び施設・経路データ」 is downloadable without an account and catalogued under CC BY 4.0.** Its actual archive contains 15,556 floor-geometry features, 423 entities, and a 2,506-node / 2,653-link navigation graph across 11 source floors. It gives substantially stronger multi-operator evidence than station-map screenshots. However, the February 2023 revision adds operating hours to a March 2020 baseline; it is not evidence that passages reflect the 2026 construction layout.
2. **ほこナビ has an E01 新宿西口 indoor package:** 225 nodes, 254 links, and four labelled GSI floor groups. It is a separate northern station, not 京王新線 新宿 and not an extension touching E27. The catalogue search did not reveal separate JR, 小田急, 京王, M08, F13, S01 or S02 indoor packages.
3. **MLIT's 2020 Shinjuku revision explicitly includes the July 2020 東西自由通路**, making it particularly valuable for correcting the Tokyo baseline. Its actual geometry and specific licence PDF require login at G空間情報センター. Only public catalogue/resource pages were inspected; no registration, login or restricted download was attempted.
4. PLATEAU's inspected `53394536` underground model is north of E27. It has useful room and surface geometry but no demonstrated routable graph or verified operator/floor labels. The citywide `bldg` and `tran` catalogue is useful exterior context, not a substitute for indoor connectivity.
5. A graph path, overlapping bounding boxes, identical station names, or matching floor numbers does **not** establish a currently public, accessible, fare-free connection. The most defensible first expansion is a small **M08 west-concourse ↔ JR 西改札 frontage** slice, subject to current passage and floor reconciliation.

## Method, scope and receipts

Primary operator, government, catalogue and licence sources were checked on 2026-10-21 JST; download timestamps are recorded in UTC, starting on 2026-10-20 UTC. [The acquisition manifest](acquisitions-2026-10-21.json) records exact request/final URLs, UTC times, byte lengths, SHA-256, failures, inspection results and historical graph-path IDs. Raw files live only under `/Users/tachikoma/Developer/gteki-raw/shinjuku/`, chiefly `w5-*` directories. Existing PLATEAU files were re-inspected and rehashed, with their prior acquisition distinguished from this turn's downloads.

Ego-lite was the preferred browser, but its startup failed within the available sandbox. Public web retrieval and ordinary HTTPS downloads supplied the evidence. Download/inspection scripts were outside both the repository and download directories, under `/tmp/gteki-w5-research/`, and executed with `python3 -I`; ZIP/JSON/CSV/XML inspection used Python's standard library. PDF text extraction was used for the published specifications and construction notice. Downloaded robots files are retained: the G空間 catalogue's disallowed query/API paths were not requested, and subsequent ほこナビ resource requests used 10-second pacing. The receipt audit shows the initial catalogue search occurred about 0.8 seconds after fetching robots.txt, before that pacing was applied; this initial interval is recorded rather than described as fully compliant. No CAPTCHA, authentication, certificate verification or bot protection was bypassed. HTTP 403, TLS failures, and HTML returned from purported ODPT JSON endpoints are recorded as limitations.

The inventory below covers the requested source families and all relevant datasets found in those searches, not a claim that every undocumented file on the internet has been found. In particular, ほこナビ's API returned zero hits for Japanese `新宿`, six for Roman `shinjuku`, and 43 package names in its package list. The six results included E01, E27, 東新宿 (outside this task), two area walking networks, and an 赤羽橋 false positive caused by a Shinjuku-named resource. Searching only the Japanese label would have missed the useful records.

**Rights vocabulary:** “Yes, conditional” means the cited data licence permits an attributed derivative/public distribution subject to its conditions; it does not certify every embedded third-party work or a safe route. “UNKNOWN” means no adequate file-specific grant was established. Publicly viewable operator diagrams are reference evidence, not automatically open data or permission to trace and redistribute their geometry. Content/survey dates, catalogue update dates and download dates are kept separate.
