# Ikebukuro walking route (phone version)

Purpose: the Ikebukuro field items from the collection list, put in one walking order with a short-on-time ladder. As of 2026-10-22. Total: 250 min of item time (175 min facts + 75 min imagery), walking between stops not included. The full list is the source of truth; if this page and [the list](../field-collection-list.md) differ, the list wins.

## Rules (same as the list; nothing loosened)

- Public areas only. No faces: wait or frame them out, and blur before sharing.
- No staff-only areas, station offices or ticket-window interiors. Never enter a staff-only door.
- No tripods. Do not block the flow. Follow staff instructions.
- Contact no one. Do not ask staff anything (Owner decision, 2026-10-22).
- No measurements inside a paid area unless the stop says a ticket is needed. Nothing on this walk needs one.
- Put the date and time on every note.
- Licence: your own surface close-ups ship as CC BY 4.0, attribution "GTEki contributors" (OD-13). If a close-up unavoidably catches the edge of a logo or ad, it ships only with that region removed or blurred (OD-14).
- Any photo that frames a sign, logo or ad is a **sign photo = private evidence only**. It never ships, even blurred. Record only the wording or colours you read from it.
- Photos never prove a passage exists; they are evidence for your notes.
- Hours and lift facts you see on site are your own dated observations, not JR East data (OD-02). The Toshima draft exit mapping is alias-only (OD-22).

Photo labels below: **[private]** = sign photo, stays on your device. **[ships]** = surface close-up, no sign, logo, ad or face in frame.

## The route

Order follows the list's Suggested visit plan; nothing reordered. Stop 11 is "as you pass", not a place.

### 1. Central Passage, from the JR 中央1 gate  ★★★ (60)
- Where: Central Passage, B1, free side.
- Check: side and column of each Marunouchi gate (east, centre, west) and the Tobu 中央1 / 中央2 gates; the two floor openings near the Marunouchi gates; printed gate-name plates (Metro 中央通路西改札 versus 東武百貨店 中央連絡西改札).
- Photos: gate-name plates **[private]**.
- Minutes: 10
- Unlocks: `gap-metro-tobu-gates`; the 5 gate POIs.
- Items: A1, A2, A3

### 2. Passage west of Tobu 中央1 toward the West Exit stairs  ★★★ (30)
- Where: passage west of the Tobu 中央1 gate, toward the West Exit stairs and Exit 19.
- Check: does the lift JR calls 西口方面階段裏エレベーター exist; which levels; posted hours; which exit number or sign it names.
- Photos: the lift sign **[private]**.
- Minutes: 5
- Unlocks: `gap-step-free` (west); `exit-19`.
- Items: FC-IK-01

### 3. West Exit hall and west passage  ★★★ (30) / ★★ (90)
- Where: West Exit hall and the west passage toward Exit 8.
- Check: Exit 9 stair, which side and how many columns from Exit 10's stair, and the wording at its head. Where the stairs of exits 14, 16, 19 start; the number on the south-west stair and on the short stair beside the wide Fukutoshin stair; whether the way to Exit 8 has no gate. Add-on: note hoarding, closures and re-routing.
- Photos: Exit 9 stair-head wording **[private]**; the rest is look and write down.
- Minutes: 5 (B2) + 10 (B3 to B6, C1) + 5 est. (`[CH-04]` add-on) = 20
- Unlocks: `exit-9`, `gap-west-stairs`; `exit-14`, `exit-16`, `exit-19`, `exit-8`.
- Items: B2, B3, B4, B5, B6, C1 (add-on `[CH-04]`, alias FC-CH-04)

### 4. Same hall, one exit stair  ★ (250)
- Where: West exit hall (any free B1 hall) and one exit stair.
- Check: floor, ceiling, handrail, stair nosing.
- Photos: FC-IMG-04 **[ships]**, close-ups only. The list says this also fills the missing "east/west underground plaza" photo gap.
- Minutes: 20
- Unlocks: rank 4 (IK-12), imagery.
- Items: FC-IMG-04

### 5. Passage south to the West Exit (South) hall; stairs signed 4 and 5  ★★★ (30) / ★★ (125)
- Where: passage north of the West Exit (South) hall; the stairs signed 4 and 5 (do FC-RT-03 together with D1; exit 5 is in both).
- Check: exits 5, 6a, 6b, 7: east or west side, and which column from the north edge of the south hall. Exit number on each plate for 4 and 5; whether "(new)" / "(old 4)" wording remains.
- Photos: none kept. Copy the plate text; count columns.
- Minutes: 10 (D1) + 15 (FC-RT-03) = 25
- Unlocks: `gap-west-corridor-exits`; `exit-5`, `exit-6a`, `exit-6b`, `exit-7`; name check against the Toshima draft (alias-only, OD-22).
- Items: D1, FC-RT-03

### 6. South Passage; JR west link; South Passage near exit 41  ★★★ (60) / ★★ (90)
- Where: South Passage; the JR west passage link; South Passage near exit 41 (flight `s41_j` to `s41_f`).
- Check: side and column of the Yurakucho South Passage west and east gates, counted from the JR South Gate; whether both ends of the JR-side link have gate lines and whether it can be walked without a ticket. Near exit 41: ramp, slope, lift or only steps; step count; sign wording.
- Photos: gate signs **[private]**; the exit-41 flight is look, count, write down.
- Minutes: 10 (F2, F3) + 5 est. (FC-SH-02) = 15
- Unlocks: `gap-south-west`, `gap-tobu-jr-link`; `gap-step-free` (#203 A1(b), I12). The 南通路西改札 to 4番出入口 POI addition is approved; F2 still gives the gate's side and column.
- Items: F2, F3, FC-SH-02

### 7. Central Passage east; East B1 hall toward exit 35  ★★★ (60) / ★★ (90)
- Where: east of the Marunouchi east gate (中央通路東改札), exits 34 and 35 stair heads; then the East B1 hall toward exit 35 (`e_hall` to `e_low`).
- Check: which exit number the lift surfaces at and which the escalator (the Metro pages disagree: 34 or 35); wording at both stair heads. At the hall: ramp, slope, lift or only steps; step count; sign wording. Do not pick 34 or 35 in data until done.
- Photos: both stair-head signs **[private]**.
- Minutes: 10 (FC-IK-02, includes FC-SH-03) + 5 est. (FC-SH-01) = 15
- Unlocks: `gap-step-free` (east); #203 A1(a), I12.
- Items: FC-IK-02, FC-SH-01

### 8. ISP and Seibu side  ★★ (125)
- Where: ISP, the slope beside the Starbucks 南館 shop and the central-passage-side entrance; Seibu 1F gate frontage, stair 36 and the 東口（南） sign.
- Check: where the slope starts and ends; exit number on the plate. Printed gate name; whether signs say 36番 and 東口（南） (names only; alias, OD-22).
- Photos: none. Write down the wording.
- Minutes: 10 est. (FC-SH-04) + 5 est. (FC-SH-05) = 15
- Unlocks: `gap-step-free`, `gap-ne-north`; Seibu name and link check.
- Items: FC-SH-04, FC-SH-05

### 9. North-east, shopping-park passage  ★★ (90)
- Where: end of the shopping-park passage in the north-east; the east side of that block.
- Check: is the stair at the passage end 24 or 25 and where is the other; can 23, 29, 30, 31 be reached from the underground passage and where do they start; are 30 and 31 one stairway with two numbers or two stairways.
- Photos: stair signs **[private]**.
- Minutes: 15
- Unlocks: `gap-ne-north`; `exit-23`, `exit-24`, `exit-25`, `exit-29`, `exit-30`, `exit-31`.
- Items: G1, G2 (alias FC-IK-03)

### 10. East side, freshness walk-throughs  ★ (175)
- Where: stairs 36 and 37 and the Seibu / Yodobashi passage; then exits C1b, 2b, 1b (positions not stated in the sources; find them by sign), plus 1a and C4.
- Check: escalators or lifts added or not; exit positions. For C1b, 2b, 1b: open or closed, lift or not, exit sign. For 1a and C4: the posted time-limit text.
- Photos: none listed. Dated walk-through; free area only; copy posted text.
- Minutes: 25 (FC-CH-02) + 25 (FC-CH-03: 20 + 5 est.) = 50
- Unlocks: `gap-freshness`, `gap-step-free`; rank 14; the open / closed gap Metro's list leaves.
- Items: FC-CH-02, FC-CH-03 (includes FC-RT-04)

### 11. Throughout: as you pass  ★★★ (30, H1/H2) / ★★ (125, FC-SH-06) / ★ (250, imagery)
- Where: Central, South and North B1 passages, at the places that fit.

Imagery, all **[ships]** and surface only (no sign, logo, ad or face; ceiling shot straight up): FC-IMG-01 floor tile, flat and straight down, ruler or coin for scale, plus one wide floor-and-ceiling frame per passage (15); FC-IMG-02 ceiling panel grid, joints, fittings (10); FC-IMG-03 walls and columns with an object for scale (10); FC-IMG-08 tactile paving where it meets the tile (10).
