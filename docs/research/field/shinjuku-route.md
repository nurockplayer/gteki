# Shinjuku walking route (phone version)

Purpose: the Shinjuku field items from the collection list, put in one walking order with a short-on-time ladder. As of 2026-10-22. Total: 95 min core walk, 180 min with the free extensions, and a separate 265 min ticket visit (paid). Walking between stops not included. The full list is the source of truth; if this page and [the list](../field-collection-list.md) differ, the list wins.

## Rules (same as the list; nothing loosened)

- Public areas only. No faces: wait or frame them out, and blur before sharing.
- No staff-only areas, station offices or ticket-window interiors. Never enter a staff-only door.
- No tripods. Do not block the flow. Follow staff instructions.
- Contact no one. Do not ask staff anything (Owner decision, 2026-10-22).
- No measurements inside a paid area unless the stop says a ticket is needed. Only the ticket visit at the end does.
- Put the date and time on every note.
- Licence: your own surface close-ups ship as CC BY 4.0, attribution "GTEki contributors" (OD-13). If a close-up unavoidably catches the edge of a logo or ad, it ships only with that region removed or blurred (OD-14).
- Any photo that frames a sign, logo or ad is a **sign photo = private evidence only**. It never ships, even blurred. Record only the wording or colours you read from it.
- Photos never prove a passage exists; they are evidence for your notes.
- No JR East data (OD-02). Facts you see on site are your own dated observations.

Photo labels below: **[private]** = sign photo, stays on your device. **[ships]** = surface close-up, no sign, logo, ad or face in frame.

## If you only have N minutes

Ranked by what each stop unlocks per minute. Each rung adds to the one before. Walk the stops in route order and skip those not in your rung.

**30 min (★★★)**
- FC-SJ-01 (20): `gap:closures`, `gap:exit-numbers`; rank 14. Is exit 2 still closed.
- FV-E27-04 (10): the E27 look (final source, OD-30); rank 4.

**55 min (★★)** adds 25
- FC-IMG-07 (25): E27 / E01 free-side surfaces; rank 4; the E27 look (final source, OD-30).

**95 min (★★)** adds 40, the core walk complete
- FC-SX-05 (15): the zh/ko name gap.
- FC-IMG-05 (15) and FC-IMG-06 (10): surfaces, rank 4 only.

**140 min (★)** adds 45, free
- FC-SJ-02 (45): `gap:exit-numbers`, `gap:hours`; rank 14; the per-exit table; the six E27 exit points.

**180 min (★)** adds 40, free
- FC-CH-01 (40): `gap:closures`; SJ-05 freshness; rank 14. Best done at stop 1, same plaza.

**Ticket visit, +265 min (★)**: one fare, separate trip, see the end.

## The route

Order follows the list's Suggested visit plan. Free extensions are slotted into the same walk where they sit (stop 1b and stop 6) and are marked.

### 1. West underground plaza, free side  ★★ (95)
- Where: west underground plaza, free side.
- Check: floor, ceiling, column cladding. Put the renovation state in the file name.
- Photos: FC-IMG-05 **[ships]**, surface close-ups only.
- Minutes: 15
- Unlocks: rank 4 (SJ-17).
- Items: FC-IMG-05

### 1b. West Exit B1 plaza: closures  (free extension) ★ (180)
- Where: West Exit B1 plaza, public: stairs 1, 2, 3, 11 (and temporary stairs), the north passage, the temporary lifts.
- Check: which are open or closed; level-change steps; lift positions.
- Photos: own-use walk-through photos only, private; none go into the repo.
- Minutes: 40
- Unlocks: `gap:closures`; SJ-05 freshness; rank 14.
- Items: FC-CH-01

### 2. Keio New Line approach  ★★★ (30)
- Where: Keio New Line approach, the former exit 2 site.
- Check: is exit 2 still closed; the posted notice text; which passage and gate side the closed exit adjoins.
- Photos: none kept in the repo. Write down the posted text.
- Minutes: 20
- Unlocks: `gap:closures`, `gap:exit-numbers`; rank 14.
- Items: FC-SJ-01

### 3. JR east-west free passage  ★★ (95)
- Where: the JR east-west free passage.
- Check: floor, ceiling, lighting strip.
- Photos: FC-IMG-06 **[ships]**, surface only.
- Minutes: 10
- Unlocks: rank 4 (SJ-17).
- Items: FC-IMG-06

### 4. E27 / E01 free-side concourse (outside the paid gates)  ★★ (55)
- Where: E27 / E01 free-side concourse, outside the fare gates.
- Check: floor, kick plate, line-colour band, exit stair; wall and column cladding close-ups with scale; luminaires on any lit ceiling with a white card in frame (estimates colour cast, does not measure it).
- Photos: FC-IMG-07 **[ships]**, surface close-ups only. Frame out any sign, logo, ad or face.
- Minutes: 15 + 10 est. = 25 (includes FV-E27-03, FV-E27-05)
- Unlocks: rank 4 (SJ-17); the E27 look (final source, OD-30).
- Items: FC-IMG-07

### 5. E27 signs, free side  ★★★ (30) / ★★ (95)
- Where: E27 free-side concourse, Oedo gate approach, and the E27 station-name signs.
- Check: FV-E27-04: sign ground and text colour, typeface, pictogram style, shot square-on with a colour card. FC-SX-05: languages and spellings shown (en, zh, ko).
- Photos: FV-E27-04 sign photo **[private]**; record only the colours and style. FC-SX-05: walk and write down the text.
- Minutes: 10 est. (FV-E27-04) + 15 (FC-SX-05) = 25
- Unlocks: the E27 look (final source, OD-30), rank 4 (SJ-17); the zh/ko name gap.
- Items: FV-E27-04, FC-SX-05

### 6. All street exits of E27 / S01  (free extension) ★ (140)
- Where: every street exit (A1, 1 to 8), including the six E27 exit points (`poi:exit:1` to `6`).
- Check: plate text, line tags, posted hours; adjacent street or building name; whether each opens to the street or an underground space; the device. The A1 street lift can be looked at here (+5 est.; also counts toward FC-SJ-04).
- Photos: plate photos, if any, **[private]**. Otherwise copy the text.
- Minutes: 45
- Unlocks: `gap:exit-numbers`, `gap:hours`; rank 14; the per-exit table (Q1).
- Items: FC-SJ-02 (includes FC-SX-01)

IMG-08 (tactile paving) and IMG-09 (sign style, private) are planned in the Ikebukuro walk; the list says you may repeat them here if you want. They are not counted again.

## Not on this walk

**Ticket visit (T3, optional, one fare, about 265 min).** Do all of these on one ticket. They need a paid-area entry; measure or look only in the places named.
- FC-SJ-05 (90): E27 to JR, Keio, Odakyu, S01, E01, M08. Signage wording; whether a fare gate is passed; walking time at your own pace. Unlocks `gap:transfers`, rank 14. Gates between operators.
- FC-SJ-03 (45): E27, S01, E01 accessible toilets. Floor, passage, nearest gate, inside or outside the gate, signage text. Unlocks the toilet POIs. Inside-gate toilets need a ticket.
- FC-SJ-04 (40): lifts at A1 and the E27 / S01 platform links. Which exits have a working lift, hours, where each opens; whether A1 and `poi:exit:3` are the same lift. Unlocks `gap:heights`. Platform-side lifts need a ticket.
- FC-SJ-06 (30): Toei store entries (新宿, 新宿西口). Floor, gate side and nearest-exit text. Rank 7, text only. One B2 inside-gate entry needs a ticket.
- FC-RT-05 (30): 小田急 新宿 南口改札口 and 西口地下改札外. Which exit or ground level the lift reaches; wording of the transfer signs to 京王 and 丸ノ内線. Unlocks `gap:transfers`. Do it with FC-SJ-05. Gate side needs a ticket.
- FC-IMG-10 (optional, 30 + fare: 20 + 10 est.): E27 platform. Shoot: (a) wall and floor finish and the screen-door base; (b) a flat close-up of the grey wall panel surface with a grey or white card in frame to fix the colour; (c) one wide frame showing the wall-panel curvature; (d) the floor surface with a coin or ruler for scale; (e) the ceiling panel joints and the luminaire type. Includes FV-E27-01 and FV-E27-02. Rank 4 and the E27 look (final source, OD-30). Photos **[ships]**, surface only, no faces, ads or signs.
- FC-CH-05 (0): Keio B2 platform, north end. Nothing until about 2030; deferred. Check later.

**Blocked (T4), skip, contact forbidden:** Shinjuku S10 entrance coordinates (needs the data owner, 東京都交通局; FC-SJ-06 only gives text), and the both-station rows (floor-to-elevation mapping, paid / unpaid boundaries and current hours, measuring or scanning on the premises). They need an operator or a permitted survey.
