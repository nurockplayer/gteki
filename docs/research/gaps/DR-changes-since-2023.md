## 3. Findings notes (facts only)

1. Ikebukuro: the ward's underground guide map (DOS:I-05) page, updated 2025-08-04, still lists 令和3年10月 and 令和4年1月 as the latest revisions of the guide map. No later revision is recorded on that page. [DR-CH-20]
2. Ikebukuro: the ward's station-area committee says the sign plan is updated about every two years, and its 2024-11 and 2026-03 meetings carry an agenda item for changes to the terminal signs reported by Tokyo Metro. The 2026-03-25 item's document (資料2) was not opened, so its content (exit or passage changes) is UNKNOWN. [DR-CH-21, DR-CH-27]
3. Ikebukuro: 池袋駅西口地区 had its city plan decided on 2024-11-11; the ward's own materials set the project period to FY2043. That is before-construction status; no passage or exit closure tied to it was found in the pages read. [DR-CH-24, DR-CH-26]
4. Ikebukuro: Tobu's own schedule (secondary) puts construction start at FY2030, after FY2027 approvals; earlier reports gave FY2027 as demolition start. The two differ, so the start of any work that touches passages is not settled. [DR-CH-33, DR-CH-29]
5. Shinjuku: the Tokyo portal lists five passage changes and four equipment changes as "completed", all dated 2024-03-15 or 2024-11-22 as posting dates, with the actual change dates in 2023 to 2024 (CH-SJ-01 to 05). [DR-CH-02, DR-CH-04, DR-CH-05]
6. Shinjuku: dated changes after the 2025-09 snapshot of the E27 data: 2025-09-27 (ground), 2026-02-17 (ground), 2026-04-07 (B1), 2026-06-23 (stair 11). Changes before it: 2023-02 to 2025-08. Whether the Hokonavi E27 files reflect them is not established by any source read. [DR-CH-02, DR-CH-07, DR-CH-08, DR-CH-09, DR-CH-10]
7. Shinjuku: the portal states that the list of "usable elevators" changed and that some sidewalks are narrowed; completion years for the plaza underground works are not stated on the portal or in the design-principles page. [DR-CH-02, DR-CH-12]
8. Shinjuku: the portal labels the Tokyo Metro/Toei access paths in B1 (route videos from the Metro gate, the 3番階段 area, and the 16/17番階段 area to bus stops 2/3). Which of these correspond to E27 exits is not stated. [DR-CH-02]
