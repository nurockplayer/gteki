The Tobu page was read directly as public HTML after the fetch service timed out. Its terms basis is the existing OD-18 decision and DR-retry-unread-sources.md, not a newly granted licence. The tourism booklet's publisher is independent of Tobu department store. **West: confirmed (two independent written sources, not field-checked). East: unverified**: only the booklet states the store is on the east side; the ward guideline supports Seibu-named East Exit entrances without naming the store. The east label visibly carries 未確認 / unconfirmed. Neither side is omitted. To confirm east, a source independent of 豊島区 that names 西武池袋本店 on the East Exit side is needed.

The shipped plain labels 東武百貨店 / Tobu department store and 西武池袋本店 / Seibu Ikebukuro main store name the department stores these sources describe (renamed from the bare operator names after the Ikebukuro route lane's review, so they are not read as railway facts). They do not certify railway gate locations or routes. No arrows, exit-number mapping, draft layout, Tobu Railway gate name or line badge is used. Railway-side topology is not inferred from a diagram.
