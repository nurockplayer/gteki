The Tobu page was read directly as public HTML after the fetch service timed out. Its terms basis is the existing OD-18 decision and DR-retry-unread-sources.md, not a newly granted licence. The tourism booklet's publisher is independent of Tobu department store. **West: confirmed** (東武百貨店 + the booklet). **East: confirmed** (the booklet + OpenStreetMap, which is independent of 豊島区; the ward guideline is supporting only). All are desk sources, not field-checked. Neither side is omitted.

The shipped plain labels 東武百貨店 / Tobu department store and 西武池袋本店 / Seibu Ikebukuro main store name the department stores these sources describe (renamed from the bare operator names after the Ikebukuro route lane's review, so they are not read as railway facts). They do not certify railway gate locations or routes. No arrows, exit-number mapping, draft layout, Tobu Railway gate name or line badge is used. Railway-side topology is not inferred from a diagram.
