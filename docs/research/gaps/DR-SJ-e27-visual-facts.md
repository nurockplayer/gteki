## 3. Findings

Format: fact, then [source_id], then date and an "outdated" flag where the item is older than the 2025-09 E27 snapshot.

### 3.1 Station facts that bear on materials

- E27 is a single island platform with two tracks, on the 7th basement level under a prefectural road, in 渋谷区代々木. Platform level was built by shield tunnelling, upper levels by cut and cover. [DR-VF-03] Structure facts, unlikely to change.
- Opened 1997-12-19 as 12号線 (single-track parallel running with 都庁前 until the 2000-04-20 extension). [DR-VF-03] Depth value given for the line article: 36.6 m. [DR-VF-02]
- The Oedo loop's interior design programme (designers chosen by proposal, treated as public art) covered 26 loop stations and excluded 都庁前 and 新宿. [DR-VF-02] Secondary; one reading is that E27 finishes came from the civil-works package, but no source says so, so treat that as open.
- Concourse layout as described by Wikipedia: the gate facing JR-direction passage at B1 (opposite the Keio New Line gate); the 3F-underground (B3) gate toward Maynds Tower with a lift from the platform end. [DR-VF-03] Layout facts only; not evidence of passages.
- Toei page for 新宿 (Oedo side): 16 escalators, 2 lifts, step-free route secured, accessible toilet present; address 渋谷区代々木2-1-1. FY2024 average daily boardings 62,465 and alightings 66,475 (Oedo). No finish information on the page. [DR-VF-01] Read 2026-10-22; current to the 2025-09 snapshot or newer.

### 3.3 Signage system

- **Line colour name:** マゼンタ; line symbol letter E (from "Oedo", second letter used because O reads like 0). [DR-VF-02] Name spellings vary (マゼンダ in news; ルビー in one cited source). [DR-VF-02, DR-VF-07]
- **Line colour decision:** 1986-06-30 per a Wikipedia timeline citing the Toei 90-year history. [DR-VF-02] Secondary. Search summary of a traffic-news article says Toei and the then-Eidan agreed line colours in 1970, taking car-body colour as the basis, and that the 12号線 colour was added in 1985 with the plan. [DR-VF-07] (summary only, article not read in full; about 2020).
- **Numeric value:** none from Toei or Tokyo. `#b6007a` is Wikipedia's infobox value, with no source for it. Treat as unverified placeholder only. [DR-VF-02]
- **Original sign style:** black background with green or white characters, unique to Oedo among Toei lines; updated from about 2010 line-wide to a style close to Tokyo Metro's; the macron on romanised names was dropped with the update. Few originals remain. [DR-VF-02] Secondary, undated beyond "around 2010", so whether E27 has fully switched is not established.
- **Tokyo terminal-station sign guideline (Urban Development Bureau PDF made 2017-12):** text in Japanese with English; pictograms per the standard guide symbols (Eco-Mo Foundation, 2015-05) and Tokyo's 2015-02 guideline; line marks and station numbering may be used; typeface basis is square gothic; figure and ground should differ strongly in lightness; safety colours and exit colours follow JIS; colours should be unified across operators where possible. It uses 新宿 examples, including a sample with 都営大江戸線新宿西口駅. [DR-VF-05] This is a guideline, not a Toei specification, and the Shinjuku case it describes is the terminal-wide scheme.
- **Tokyo 2015-02 revision summary:** Japanese and English as the base, pictograms used, key rail facilities (exits, gates, ticket machines, information) preferably in four languages, line marks and station numbering used for clarity. [DR-VF-06]
- **Pictogram standard:** the standard guide symbols are the pictogram basis in both Tokyo documents. JIS Z 8210 is not named in them. Whether Toei's own signs use JIS Z 8210 pictograms is not established by any Toei statement found.
- **Typeface:** see section 2. No Toei sign manual or procurement specification was found.
- **Use restriction to note:** Toei's route-map loan terms forbid altering the text or colours of the loaned map (search summary of the Toei map-loan page, not opened). This bears on copying Toei map colours into art, not on a plain colour fact. [DR-VF-11]
