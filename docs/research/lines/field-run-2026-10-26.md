# Field run 2026-10-26: what the desk could not settle

**For:** the Owner's visit. **Written:** 2026-10-25 by `ds-research` (deepseek-flash) for data-research-05. **Docs only: no station data, graph, POI or import was changed.**

**What is here.** Everything a lookup could settle sits in `docs/research/field/ikebukuro-online-closures-2026-10-25.md` (#512, open) and the original walking frame in [`ikebukuro-field-checklist.md`](../field/ikebukuro-field-checklist.md) (#493, on `main`). This sheet keeps **only the residue**: #512's FIELD and PARTIAL items with the open part only, the still-open field rows of [`gap-table-2026-10-25.md`](gap-table-2026-10-25.md) (#501), the step-free gap (`gap-step-free`, no lift edges in the graph) and the gate-connector gaps. #512's CLOSED rows are dropped (its rows 1, 2 and 28: Exit 22's printed name, the other five exit names, PARCO floors).

**Two loops.** The public-area loop is **19 items in about 80 minutes**; the paid-area loop is **5 items in about 40–55 minutes and needs a ticket**, split into three operator subloops — JR, Tokyo Metro and Tobu — because each is a separate fare. They are separate walks because a ticket is needed for the second.

**Sequence, public loop.** Starts at the 西口（地下）hall (node `w_c`, POI `west-exit-b1`) and ends at the Exit 22 street landing (node `x22`, POI `exit-22`) — both named public points. `w_c` → South Passage (`w_cj` → `sw_h` → `sp_m` → `g_jr_s`) and back → West Passage (`w_cj` → `w_13j` → `w_w` → `w_nw`) and back → `w_c` → 中央通路 east (`c_w` → `c_mid` → `c_g2` → `c_c` → `c_e1` → `c_n`) → north run (`n_h`) → いけふくろう → 北通路 (`n_c`) → up the Exit 22 stair (`x22`). Every edge is walked from the free side only: items 7 and 10 are the checks that may show an edge is inside a gate line — stop there instead of crossing (see the rules). Items 18 and 19 are filled in as you walk, not at an extra stop.

**How to read an item.** One item = one gap. "CLOSED when" is the test the Owner applies on the spot; write the date and time on every note. A field note is a dated observation, not a new public source: it confirms what you see, but it does not by itself raise a fact's source count — the data lane decides what gets imported.

## Rules for the visit

From #493 §2 and §4:

- **Public areas only**, unless an item says **needs a ticket**. No track areas, no staff-only rooms, no station offices.
- **Do not cross a gate line without a ticket.** Every gate named below is a fare boundary; all public-loop checks are made from the free side. If a gate line turns up across an edge the loop walks, stop on the free side, record it against item 7 or item 10, and go round — that edge is not walked through, and the loop continues from the last free-side point. **The two named free-side ways round** are the Exit 4 landing `x4f` (POI `exit-4` 「西口（南）」) to the street, and the Exit 22 stair `x22` (POI `exit-22`) between the street and `n_c`; walk between the two at street level. Nothing in the data proves a connector passable: where print and data leave a gate line or an opening unplaced, the check is made from the last free-side point, and every item on a skipped edge is recorded PARTIAL with the reason.
- **Ask no one anything, sign nothing, fill in no form, post nothing.** Follow staff instructions; no tripods; do not block the flow.
- **Photos are private evidence and are never committed** — not to the repo, not to the product, not to a PR, not blurred. Sign photos (gate plates, exit plates, posted hours) and the いけふくろう photo stay on the Owner's device or private store. Only the fact read from a photo is written into a note.
- No faces in frame, no staff, no gate-line interiors, no track areas. Wait or reframe.
- Record the date and time on every note.

## Paid-area loop (5 items, 3 operator subloops, ~40–55 min plus walking between gates, **needs a ticket**)

Three subloops, one fare each — JR, Tokyo Metro and Tobu are separate operators, and each subloop enters and leaves the paid area by the gates named in its heading. Do the ones you buy for; leave the rest undone rather than force it. Inside the gates the same photo rules apply: plates only, no faces, no staff, no track-side lingering.
