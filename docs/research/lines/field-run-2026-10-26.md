# Field run 2026-10-26: what the desk could not settle

**For:** the Owner's visit. **Written:** 2026-10-25 by `ds-research` (deepseek-flash) for data-research-05. **Docs only: no station data, graph, POI or import was changed.**

**What is here.** Everything a lookup could settle sits in `docs/research/field/ikebukuro-online-closures-2026-10-25.md` (#512, open) and the original walking frame in [`ikebukuro-field-checklist.md`](../field/ikebukuro-field-checklist.md) (#493, on `main`). This sheet keeps **only the residue**: #512's FIELD and PARTIAL items with the open part only, the still-open field rows of [`gap-table-2026-10-25.md`](gap-table-2026-10-25.md) (#501), the step-free gap (`gap-step-free`, no lift edges in the graph) and the gate-connector gaps. #512's CLOSED rows are dropped (its rows 1, 2 and 28: Exit 22's printed name, the other five exit names, PARCO floors).

**Two loops.** The public-area loop is **19 items in about 80 minutes**; the paid-area loop is **5 items in about 45–60 minutes and needs a ticket**. They are separate walks because a ticket is needed for the second.

**Sequence, public loop.** Starts at the 西口（地下）hall (node `w_c`, POI `west-exit-b1`) and ends at the Exit 22 street landing (node `x22`, POI `exit-22`) — both named public points. `w_c` → South Passage (`w_cj` → `sw_h` → `sp_m` → `g_jr_s`) and back → West Passage (`w_cj` → `w_13j` → `w_w` → `w_nw`) and back → `w_c` → 中央通路 east (`c_w` → `c_mid` → `c_g2` → `c_c` → `c_e1` → `c_n`) → north run (`n_h`) → いけふくろう → 北通路 (`n_c`) → up the Exit 22 stair (`x22`). Every edge is walked from the free side only: items 7 and 10 are the checks that may show an edge is inside a gate line — stop there instead of crossing (see the rules). Items 18 and 19 are filled in as you walk, not at an extra stop.

**How to read an item.** One item = one gap. "CLOSED when" is the test the Owner applies on the spot; write the date and time on every note. A field note is a dated observation, not a new public source: it confirms what you see, but it does not by itself raise a fact's source count — the data lane decides what gets imported.

## Rules for the visit

From #493 §2 and §4:

- **Public areas only**, unless an item says **needs a ticket**. No track areas, no staff-only rooms, no station offices.
- **Do not cross a gate line without a ticket.** Every gate named below is a fare boundary; all public-loop checks are made from the free side. If a gate line turns up across an edge the loop walks, stop on the free side, record it against item 7 or item 10, and go round on the free side — that edge is not walked through, and the loop continues from the last free-side point.
- **Ask no one anything, sign nothing, fill in no form, post nothing.** Follow staff instructions; no tripods; do not block the flow.
- **Photos are private evidence and are never committed** — not to the repo, not to the product, not to a PR, not blurred. Sign photos (gate plates, exit plates, posted hours) and the いけふくろう photo stay on the Owner's device or private store. Only the fact read from a photo is written into a note.
- No faces in frame, no staff, no gate-line interiors, no track areas. Wait or reframe.
- Record the date and time on every note.

## Public-area loop (19 items, ~80 min, no ticket)

### Stop 1 — 西口（地下）hall · node `w_c` · 3 min

**1. Is 「西口（地下）」 printed anywhere?**
**Where:** node `w_c`, POI `west-exit-b1` 「西口（地下）」/「West Exit (B1)」.
**Look for:** the hall's name plates and the exit signs over the west doors; copy the exact wording. Is 「西口（地下）」 on any plate, or does the hall carry a different proper name? Is the lift the ward calls 「西口五差路 EV」 labelled here?
**CLOSED when:** the hall plate's exact wording is written down — either 「西口（地下）」 or the name that replaces it.
**Field:** `west-exit-b1` name/alias. **Why not online:** no printed source uses 「西口（地下）」（#512 row 3, PARTIAL). **Gap:** G12.

### Stop 2 — South Passage · edges `sp_m__sw_h` (48.7 m), `sp_m__g_jr_s` (9.7 m), `sw_h__x4f` · 12 min

Walk `w_c` → `w_cj` → `sw_h` → `sp_m` → `g_jr_s` (JR 南改札), reading the south side.

**2. The two Yurakucho gate lines and their fare side.**
**Where:** `sp_m__sw_h` and `sp_m__g_jr_s`, south side; POIs `metro-y-south-west-gate` 「有楽町線 南通路西改札」 and `metro-y-south-east-gate` 「有楽町線 南通路東改札」; reference gate `g_jr_s` 「JR 南改札」.
**Look for:** the plate wording and the gate line of each; count structural columns from the JR 南改札 line to each plate; which side of each line is the free passage, and does any sign print 改札外.
**CLOSED when:** each plate's position is written as "N columns / ≈N m east|west of JR 南改札, north|south side" and the free side is named.
**Field:** `connectorNodeIds` for both Y09 gates; `accessFacts` fare side. **Why not online:** Metro prints only the names, and the gate machines sit inside surveyed floor with no gate-line opening (#512 rows 23, 24). **Gap:** `gap-south-west`, F2, I8.

**3. The 南通路西改札 lift/escalator to Exit 4 (エソラ).**
**Where:** near `sw_h`; Exit 4 foot `x4f` on `sw_h__x4f`, POI `exit-4` 「4番出口」/「西口（南）」.
**Look for:** the lift and escalator Metro prints as 「南通路西改札～4番出入口（エソラ）」: where they stand, that the lift's door panel lists **B1 and 1F**, and whether the way to Exit 4 stays outside the gates.
**CLOSED when:** the lift is seen with its floor list, the gate line it sits outside of is named, and the note says whether the way from that lift to Exit 4 stays outside the gates (or where it does not).
**Field:** lift POI; `accessFacts` fare side for Exit 4. **Why not online:** the link is printed but its position and fare side are not (#512 row 24). **Gap:** G2, I8.

**Where:** `w_13j__w_w`, `w_w__w_nw`; POIs `metro-f-west-east-gate` 「副都心線 西通路東改札」 and `metro-f-west-west-gate` 「副都心線 西通路西改札」 (both SEARCH_ONLY, no connector).
**Look for:** both plates; count columns; which is nearer the マルイシティ出入口 and which nearer C9/C1b; the free side of each.
**CLOSED when:** each plate's position (columns from a named reference) and its free side are written.
**Field:** `connectorNodeIds` for both F09 gates; `accessFacts`. **Why not online:** only the names are printed; no position or fare side exists anywhere read (#512 row 19). **Gap:** F09 "no geometry source" (#501), connector gap.

**5. The C exits and the C9/C1b lifts — a geometry note.**
**Where:** the same passage, west of `w_nw`; printed pairs 「西通路東改札～C9番出入口」 and 「西通路東改札～C1b出入口」.
**Look for:** the C exits you pass, in order, with the number over each and its side; where C1b and C9 stand relative to the 西通路東改札 plate; the lift for C9, with its floor list.
**CLOSED when:** a dated list of the C exits in order with side and number exists, and the C9 lift's floors are read.
**Field:** a field geometry note for the F09 side (input to a future survey, not an import); lift POI candidate for C9. **Why not online:** Echika and the C exits are absent from PLATEAU 13116 and no printed geometry exists (#512 row 19). **Gap:** F09 "no geometry source" (#501); G2 for the lift.

### Stop 5 — 中央通路 centre · edges `c_w__c_mid` (37.3), `c_mid__c_g2` (11.4), `c_g2__c_c` (21.3), `c_c__c_e1` (33.2), `c_e1__c_n` (17.8) · 15 min

**8. Steps, thresholds and the narrowest point on the five central edges.**
**Where:** the five edges above, through `c_mid`, `c_g2` (JR 中央2改札 mouth), `c_c`, `c_e1`. All are `wheelchair: null`.
**Look for:** any step, threshold or slope on a nominally flat passage — count steps and name the edge; the narrowest point and roughly how wide; whether a wheelchair could pass the whole run.
**CLOSED when:** each of the five edges is written "flat, no step", "N steps at X", "slope at X" or "threshold at X" — a slope or a lip is recorded as such and never rounded to "flat" — plus one narrowest-point note.
**Field:** `wheelchair`/`steps` on the five edges. **Why not online:** no source prints step-free facts for these edges (#512 row 13). **Gap:** G2, `gap-step-free`.

**9. 中央通路東改札 and its lift: does it reach 34 or 35?**
**Where:** POI `metro-m-central-east-gate` 「丸ノ内線 中央通路東改札」, near `c_mid`/`c_c`; printed links 「中央通路東改札～34番出入口」 (lift) and 「中央通路東改札～35番出入口」 (escalator); the 改札外広場 with the 改札外 toilet.
**Look for:** the gate plate; where the 改札外 lift and escalator stand relative to it; ride the lift and read the exit number at the street landing — 34 or 35 (the sources disagree); any posted escalator service hours.
**CLOSED when:** the ground lift is tied to a printed exit number seen at its top landing, plus the gate plate's wording and position.
**Field:** `metro-m-central-east-gate` connector; lift POI; escalator service hours (as service hours, not passage hours). **Why not online:** Metro names both ends, but its exit list puts the ground lift on 35, not 34 (#512 rows 14, 16; FC-IK-02). **Gap:** G2, FC-IK-02.

### Stop 6 — North run and JR 北改札 · edge `c_n__n_h` (75.8 m), POI `jr-north-gate` · 15 min

**10. Is the free passage continuous to 北通路, and where does JR 北改札 open onto it?**
**Where:** edge `c_n__n_h`, nodes `c_n` and `n_h`, POI `jr-north-gate` 「JR 北改札」 (no connector).
**Look for:** whether the passage runs unbroken north along the east side of the JR concourse to 北通路, outside every gate; the exact spot the JR 北改札 line opens onto it — side, number of bays, columns from `c_n` or `n_h`; the plate wording.
**CLOSED when:** the gate line's position on the passage (side + columns from a named node) and the continuity of the free passage are both written.
**Field:** `jr-north-gate` connector; `accessFacts` (`af-east-jr-passage-north`). **Why not online:** the printed sources give a walk out of the gate, not the opening, and never state the connection (#512 rows 6, 21). **Gap:** G4, A2, FC-IK-01.

**11. Tobu 北改札 — does it exist on this side?**
**Where:** the north side of the same passage; POI `tobu-north-gate` 「東武東上線 北改札」 (printed only by the 2022 ward map, no position).
**Look for:** any Tobu gate plate on this side, and if found, where it opens.
**CLOSED when:** the plate is found and its position written — or "no Tobu 北改札 seen on this side (inspected, not found)". The second answer closes the inspection only: the gate's location, its connector and the item stay UNKNOWN.
**Field:** `tobu-north-gate` connector. **Why not online:** nothing printed places the Tobu gates or their opening on a named passage (#512 row 27). **Gap:** A2-equivalent (#512 row 27).

**12. Lifts on the north run and at JR 北改札.**
**Where:** `c_n`, `c_n__n_h`, `n_h`, `jr-north-gate`.
**Look for:** every lift; the floors its panel lists; whether it sits on this route or off it; whether any lift serves 北改札; and whether the run itself is step-free (any step, threshold or slope on `c_n__n_h`).
**CLOSED when:** either a lift with its floor list is recorded here, or "no lift seen between c_n and n_h and at JR 北改札", and in both cases the note says whether the run itself is step-free — a lift nearby, or none, does not by itself set `wheelchair`.
**Field:** lift POIs; `wheelchair` on `c_n__n_h`. **Why not online:** no printed lift sits anywhere on `w_c`→`x22` or at JR 北改札 (#512 row 14). **Gap:** G2.

**13. いけふくろう — position, not name.** (same stop, free side of JR 北改札)
**Where:** free side by `jr-north-gate`, between it and `n_h`; the statue has no node or POI.
**Look for:** stand where a passenger leaving 北改札 stands, facing away from the paid area, then turn right; record which side of the passage it is on, how many columns from the gate line, whether it is at the foot of the street stairs or beside their mouth, which named element it is nearest (`n_h`, `c_n`, 北改札), and whether any plate names it 「いけふくろう」.
**CLOSED when:** side + column distance from the 北改札 line + nearest named element are written.
**Field:** landmark note for G11 (the data lane decides whether it becomes a POI). **Why not online:** sources give a route and a name, never a position (#512 row 8). **Gap:** G11.

### Stop 8 — Exit 22 stair · edge `n_c__x22` (11.0 m, stairs) · 8 min

**Where:** `n_c__x22`; POI `exit-22` 「22番出口」/「東口（北）」.
**Look for:** the plate at foot and head of the stair — does it print 22 and/or 東口（北）? Is this the stair that faces いけふくろう?
**CLOSED when:** a plate on this stair prints 22 (or 東口（北）) — or it does not, and the 22 belongs to another stair.
**Field:** edge identity (`n_c__x22` as Exit 22). **Why not online:** two sources are consistent with it but neither places the stair on `n_c` (#512 row 9). **Gap:** G5.
**If not:** items 16 and 17 are recorded for this stair as an unidentified stair, and `exit-22`'s step, lift and landing facts stay open until the real Exit 22 stair is found.

**Where:** `n_c__x22`; edge tag `stairs`, `wheelchair: false`.

**CLOSED when:** the step count is written, and either "no lift/escalator at Exit 22" or the device's position and floors.
**Field:** `steps`/`wheelchair` on the edge; lift POI if one exists. **Why not online:** no printed step count and no printed lift or escalator at Exit 22 (#512 rows 10, 13). **Gap:** G6, G2.

## Paid-area loop (5 items, ~45–60 min, **needs a ticket**)

Each operator needs its own ticket — JR, Tokyo Metro and Tobu are separate fares. Do the operators you buy for; leave the rest undone rather than force it. Inside the gates the same photo rules apply: plates only, no faces, no staff, no track-side lingering.

**20. JR East platform numbers and platform access.**
**Where:** paid side through `jr-north-gate` (no graph node); no JR platform POI exists yet.
**Look for:** the 番線 number and the line/direction at the head of every platform (or on the stair/escalator down); which gate and stair serve which island.
**CLOSED when:** every JR platform reached prints a number and its line/direction is written beside it, and the note says how many JR platforms or islands the signage shows — if that count is not established, the result is PARTIAL and the gap stays open.
**Field:** new JR platform POIs (number ↔ line/direction). **Why not online:** no printed list of JR platforms at Ikebukuro exists at any source grade (#512 row 26). **Gap:** #501 Table A, JR "no platform source".

**21. Tokyo Metro M25 platform numbers.**
**Where:** inside the gates of `metro-m-central-west-gate` / `metro-m-central-gate` / `metro-m-central-east-gate`, around `c_w`–`c_mid`.
**Look for:** the 1番線 / 2番線 plates and the 行き先 printed with each.
**CLOSED when:** both platform numbers and their directions are read off plates.
**Field:** M25 platform POIs. **Why not online:** the numbers appear only in a source with a flat ban (corroboration only), so they are still open (#512 row 16). **Gap:** WP2 `notFound`, FC-IK-02.

**22. Tokyo Metro Y09 platform numbers.**
**Where:** inside the gates of `metro-y-south-west-gate` / `metro-y-south-east-gate`, around `sp_m`.
**Look for:** the 番線 plates and the 行き先 for 和光市 and 新木場.
**CLOSED when:** both numbers and directions are read off plates.
**Field:** Y09 platform POIs. **Why not online:** the printed page gives directions without numbers (#512 row 17). **Gap:** F2, I8 (WP2 `notFound`).

**23. Tokyo Metro F09 platform numbers.**
**Where:** inside the gates of `metro-f-west-east-gate` / `metro-f-west-west-gate`, on the 西通路 side.
**Look for:** the 番線 plates and the 行き先 for 渋谷 and 和光市.
**CLOSED when:** both numbers and directions are read off plates.
**Field:** F09 platform POIs. **Why not online:** directions are printed, numbers are not (#512 row 18). **Gap:** WP2 `notFound`.

**24. Tobu Tojo platforms 1–5 and what each serves.**
**Where:** paid side of `tobu-central-gate-1` / `tobu-central-gate-2`.
**Look for:** the 番線 plate on each platform, whether platforms 4 and 5 exist as printed, and the destination or service printed with each number.
**CLOSED when:** each platform 1–3 has a number and a destination written beside it, and the note says whether 4 and 5 exist (seen, or not found on the paid side); if 4–5 are not reachable, the 4–5 part is PARTIAL and stays open.
**Field:** Tobu platform POIs. **Why not online:** only 1–3 sit on a facts-only source, and which platform serves which destination is printed nowhere (#512 row 20). **Gap:** #501 Table A, Tobu "platforms have no source".
