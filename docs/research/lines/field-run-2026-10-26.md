## Rules for the visit

From #493 §2 and §4:

- **Public areas only**, unless an item says **needs a ticket**. No track areas, no staff-only rooms, no station offices.
- **Do not cross a gate line without a ticket.** Every gate named below is a fare boundary; all public-loop checks are made from the free side. If a gate line turns up across an edge the loop walks, stop on the free side, record it against item 7 or item 10, and go round — that edge is not walked through, and the loop continues from the last free-side point. **The two named free-side ways round** are the Exit 4 landing `x4f` (POI `exit-4` 「西口（南）」) to the street, and the Exit 22 stair `x22` (POI `exit-22`) between the street and `n_c`; walk between the two at street level. Nothing in the data proves a connector passable: where print and data leave a gate line or an opening unplaced, the check is made from the last free-side point, and every item on a skipped edge is recorded PARTIAL with the reason.
- **Ask no one anything, sign nothing, fill in no form, post nothing.** Follow staff instructions; no tripods; do not block the flow.
- **Photos are private evidence and are never committed** — not to the repo, not to the product, not to a PR, not blurred. Sign photos (gate plates, exit plates, posted hours) and the いけふくろう photo stay on the Owner's device or private store. Only the fact read from a photo is written into a note.
- No faces in frame, no staff, no gate-line interiors, no track areas. Wait or reframe.
- Record the date and time on every note.

## S03 control points — Shinjuku (field, for Check B)

**Added:** 2026-10-26 18:5x JST by data-research-98 (lead), on the director's request of 18:5x JST. The desk found no independent control point: n = 0 in [`s03-check-b-control-2026-10-26.md`](s03-check-b-control-2026-10-26.md) (#551). These three items make the Owner's own position fixes the control. **3 items, ~20–25 min.** Items 53 and 54 are inside JR's paid area and are read on the JR arrival (see "The whole visit, in order").

**Why these three and not an underground gate.** A phone fixes its position only under open sky. An underground point such as S03's 「大江戸線改札B」 cannot be fixed without survey equipment, so it serves as a **floor/name tie** only (item 30's look-for), not as a control point. The three points below are each:

- a feature S03 prints by name or as a platform;
- a point with a geometric definition a person can stand on;
- open to the sky, or a few metres from open sky.

**What every control item records** (applies to items 52–54):

- **The fix.** The phone's own location readout (latitude, longitude **and** its stated horizontal accuracy), from any app that shows all three. No third-party map is used or recorded. Stand still for **2 minutes**, then write three readings about 30 s apart.
- **Where you stood.** The standing point relative to the defined point: distance by tape or by paces (say which) and a compass direction. Stand **on** the point if it has open sky.
- **Sky.** "open", "canopy" or "partly covered", plus the phone model.
- **Photos.** One of the defined point with its sign or plate and one straight up at the sky. They stay private, as everywhere on this sheet; only the readings are written down.
- **Time.** Date and time in JST.

**CLOSED when** (each item): three readings with stated accuracy ≤ 10 m, and the offset to the defined point. A reading above 10 m, or none, is written as such and leaves the item PARTIAL. **The data lane then compares** each fix with S03's transformed position for that feature, privately. With phone accuracy of about 3–10 m, these points can expose a gross misalignment (≫ 5 m). They cannot certify the 1.85 m-level agreement seen against E27; the director rules on what they are worth.
