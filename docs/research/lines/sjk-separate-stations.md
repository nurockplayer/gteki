# Separate stations around 新宿: connection facts

Research read 2026-10-29 02:34–02:35 JST. These are separate stations, not aliases for 新宿. A transfer designation or a nearby exit does not by itself establish a walkable segment. This is a facts-only desk pass, not a complete survey or a director decision. Sources and rights are keyed below; unspecified route attributes are UNKNOWN.

## 西武新宿

- 西武鉄道 西武新宿線, SS01 [S1; `lines-inventory.md` §1].
- **西武新宿駅 ↔ 新宿サブナード:** Seibu says the station connects to Subnade [S2]. A ward meeting record calls the Subnade–station underground passage existing and describes an existing lift in PePe to the station gate floor [W1, PENDING; historical statement]. Exact endpoints, passage hours and an end-to-end step-free route are not specified. The ward also says its main office can be reached from 西武新宿駅 via the underground Subnade and exit 15b [W2, PENDING]; 15b is the ward-office exit, not a station endpoint.
- **新宿サブナード ↔ メトロプロムナード:** Seibu describes a *new* underground passage between them as under consideration/discussion, intended to improve transfers to 新宿駅 (JR and Metro Marunouchi) [S2]. Do not model that proposed passage as open. This does not establish that no other existing path exists.
- **Official transfers:** Seibu's 「他社乗換路線」 lists JR埼京線, JR山手線, JR湘南新宿ライン, JR中央・総武各駅停車, JR中央線, 京王線, 小田急線, 東京メトロ丸ノ内線, 都営新宿線 and 都営大江戸線 [S1]. Toei E01 lists 「西武新宿線（西武新宿）」 under 「乗り換え案内」 [T1]. Neither specifies the walk, exits, street/underground split or hours. Seibu lists lifts as station equipment and 北口 hours 7:00～終電, not as attributes of this connection [S1].

## 新宿三丁目

- 東京メトロ 丸ノ内線 M09 and 副都心線 F13; 東京都交通局 都営新宿線 S02 [M1, T2].
- **新宿駅 ↔ 新宿三丁目駅:** Metro explicitly says 「地下通路でつながっています」 [M2]. It does not name the passage or endpoints, exit numbers, step-free status or hours. The ward says its main office is accessible from 新宿三丁目駅 through Subnade, exit 15b [W2, PENDING]; this does not identify the station-side route or prove a specific direct segment to 新宿駅. Metro's exit guide lists E10 with a lift to ground and nearby JR新宿駅 gates [M1]; a nearby-place listing is not an underground or step-free route to those gates.
- **Official transfers:** Toei lists Metro 丸ノ内線 M-09 and 副都心線 F-13 under 「乗り換え案内」 [T2]. Metro lists 「新宿三丁目駅｜丸ノ内線と副都心線」 and, for Metro–Toei IC transfers, 「新宿三丁目駅｜都営新宿線」 [M3]. These are transfers at 新宿三丁目, not a designation of 新宿駅 ↔ 新宿三丁目駅 as a transfer. M3's 60-minute condition concerns fare treatment, not passage opening hours.

## 新宿西口

- 東京都交通局 都営大江戸線 E01 [T1].
- **JR新宿駅西口出口 → 新宿西口駅:** Toei says to follow signs for the 大江戸線 platform from 「JR新宿駅西口出口」 to E01 [T3]. It does not state a particular D exit, passage name, underground/ground split, step-free route or hours. E01 station-wide accessibility reports four lifts and 「1ルートの確保：済み」 [T1]; neither proves this particular connection step-free.
- **Official transfers:** E01's 「乗り換え案内」 lists 「東京メトロ丸ノ内線（新宿）（M-08）」, JR lines, 「京王線（新宿）」, 「小田急線（新宿）」 and 「西武新宿線（西武新宿）」 [T1]. Metro's IC-transfer table lists 「新宿駅－新宿西口駅｜都営大江戸線」 [M3]. WP11 also records Metro's 新宿 transfer label 「新宿駅・新宿西口駅」, with no gate or passage [R1].
