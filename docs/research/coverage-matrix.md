Built 2026-11-01 11:4x JST by the data lead (data-research-05) for the Owner's goal: every line and every department store at both stations. Basis: `origin/main` at `c97c37a2` (2026-11-01 11:2x JST). Public facts only; private-tier additions are recorded in gteki-private and are not counted here.

Derived cells refreshed from public data at `556e6fab` on 2026-11-03 15:26 JST.

This is the one-screen view. The cell-by-cell evidence stays in [`gaps/coverage-sjk-ikb.md`](gaps/coverage-sjk-ikb.md) (2026-10-29) and the rights detail in [`lines/gap-table-2026-10-25.md`](lines/gap-table-2026-10-25.md); this file adds the navmesh and issue state since then.

**How the cells were filled.** `pnpm coverage:matrix` derives the public POI/tenant counts, search availability and default-profile router reachability; `pnpm coverage:matrix --check` reports drift against explicit counts and search/route assertions in these tables. CI writes that report to the step summary without blocking. Row ownership is hand-kept in `tools/coverage-matrix/rows.ts`; building POIs are counted separately from shop POIs, and Isetan sections plus brands are tenants. Shared JR gates repeat per line; graph sizes describe the public station graph only where mapped (Ikebukuro JR B1 and Shinjuku E27), not a navmesh bake or the held S03 graph. "Routed" requires a connector reachable from a distinct graph node through `@gteki/navigation` with its default profile. Source / rights, 3D and Open remain hand-written. The scene column follows the coverage table's rule: a named interior or facade counts, generic placeholder shopfronts (#584, #605) do not.

Step-free counts refreshed 2026-11-03 18:39 JST. `step-free k/n` counts successful step-free routes out of default-profile-routable ordered pairs: all routable POIs within a row, plus each row POI ↔ the station’s routable exits/platforms, deduplicated by POI ids and direction. Coincident connectors returning `start_equals_goal` are excluded; `0/0` means no qualifying pairs, not proven accessibility. The generator also prints station-grouped first blocking edges, recorded floors, nearby POIs and blocked pairs; `--check` compares the step-free mentions in Walk / navmesh or Search / route. Same-floor unknown access needs a survey, not an inferred lift.

**Columns.**
- **Source / rights**: where the public facts come from and their class (OD-34 / OD-35 tiers).
- **3D**: y = modelled, partial = some of its public area is modelled, n = none.
- **Walk / navmesh**: public walk graph and the baked navmesh paths (#669). Since #676, the live-scene bake leaves many paths incomplete (#684: Shinjuku 196 of 238 routes, Ikebukuro 542 of 1054); those draw as dashed fallbacks.
- **POIs / tenants**: what is in `pois.json` / `tenants.json`. s = search-only (no floor, no connector).
- **Search / route**: search = found by the search box; route = a route can end at it.
- **Open**: the issue, PR or field item (#533 numbering) that moves the row. **Field items are unscheduled:** the 2026-11-01 run did not take place and no date is set (Owner, via the director, 2026-11-02 04:5x JST). "field (unscheduled)" marks a row that waits on it; the field sheets stay as they are until a date is set.
