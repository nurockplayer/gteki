## Verified acquisition and scope

The manifest now has 75 sources: the original 19, the first 11 Lane D inputs, one fixed read-only GraphQL catalog POST, 14 direct GeoJSON identity inputs and the 2023 vintage package snapshot (evidence for inspect-vintages, not a POI input). 新宿 and 新宿西口 remain separate CSV resources; 新宿三丁目 was not acquired. The CKAN snapshots explicitly list the two S10 CSVs and all three S03 resources under CC BY 4.0. Resource-specific licence fields are absent/empty, rather than independently declaring a resource licence.

The S10 catalog resource dates are 2026-09-04, not a survey or freshness guarantee. Old CSV URLs without /other/, as observed in this run: 新宿: legacy URL returned HTTP 200 with SHA-256 identical to the /other/ resource. 新宿西口: legacy URL returned HTTP 200 with SHA-256 identical to the /other/ resource. S10 CSVs are Shift_JIS and include no coordinate columns. Blank spreadsheet rows are counted separately; literal location descriptions are never converted to floor or access semantics.

The S03 landing page lists publication on 2023-02-28 and says the update builds on March 2020 data. Its catalog creation dates are 2023-02-20. Catalog byte-size metadata differs from some actual PDF downloads: acquisition records retain the actual bytes without treating catalog estimates as content identity. The general bureau site copyright reservation is captured separately; Tokyo portal terms §3 explicitly give the catalog terms priority over other provider-site terms. The explicit dataset listing supplies the bounded geometry/factual-field and technical text permission. Promotional shop description prose and linked external content remain UNKNOWN. Photographs, graphics and third-party branding are outside that clearance.

The API specification links to the working [Tokyo portal terms](https://portal.data.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/terms/). Attempted terms URLs: https://catalog.data.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/terms returned HTTP 403; https://www.opendata.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/terms/ returned HTTP 404; https://www.opendata.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kiyaku.html returned HTTP 404. Those response bodies are hashed supporting documents and are not interpreted as licence clauses. The portal terms require source/author credits and identify separate treatment for logos and symbols. The CC BY deed supplies the attribution, licence-link and modification-notice requirements.

The [PLATEAU policy](https://www.mlit.go.jp/plateau/site-policy/) now labels §3 “コンテンツの利用”; the CKAN licence title still calls it “著作権について”. It defaults to PDL 1.0 in the absence of a separate rights notice and requires source and processing credits. PDL §1.2 leaves third-party rights with their owners. This does not transfer the CityGML CC BY claim to related derivatives.
