# Operator and field-survey questions

Read this static question template alongside the generated [Ikebukuro](ikebukuro.md) and [Shinjuku](shinjuku.md) UNKNOWN/HOLD packets. It supplies no station measurements, counts, permission grants or source-content findings. The generated packets remain the evidence for what each inspected source contains.

For either station, the station lead needs operator answers or permission for:

- Which operator manages each proposed passage, doorway and transfer boundary, and may that information be mapped and redistributed?
- May the team measure floor elevations, connector endpoints, usable widths, slopes and steps on the premises? Which areas, methods and equipment are permitted?
- May the team photograph or scan the intended survey area, and which recordings or derived geometry may be published? Restricted diagrams, third-party graphics, logos and identifiable bystanders require separate consideration.
- Which current paid/unpaid boundaries, transfer rules, door hours, construction diversions and accessibility conditions apply to the proposed route? What evidence and review date can the operator provide?
- Which source-specific floor labels correspond to surveyed physical levels? Request an evidenced mapping; never infer one from matching labels or assume that a label gives an elevation.
- May a source graph be connected to another operator's graph or to a surveyed entrance, and what actual endpoint/access evidence supports that connection?

For Ikebukuro, keep the PLATEAU underground geometry and historical surrounding-network questions separate. For Shinjuku, keep the Oedo E27 source area separate from other operators and station areas. Neither adjacency nor open-data availability answers these permission or connectivity questions.

This implementation does not authorize fieldwork. Routing and public-product claims remain HOLD until the lead resolves the relevant questions using current evidence.
