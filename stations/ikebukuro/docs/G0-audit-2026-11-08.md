## Method

A status is PASS only when a licence is declared **and** an evidence document, PR or ruling that checked it exists and was opened. A `REUSE.toml` entry or `pnpm audit:assets` alone never gives PASS (the audit proves declaration, not rights). "Attribution shown where": the in-app credits panel renders every source (`apps/web/src/credits.ts`: `sourceCredit` for CC BY/ODbL/PLATEAU, `factsOnlyCredit` for facts-only with the #26 wording and the decision link, `dataFileCredits` for the inlined data) and the persistent data-credit line (#187). Per-source "shown" means that mechanism.

Facts-only sources are used for facts, not works (Owner decision #26, 2026-10-21; `G0-source-audit.md` "Owner decision"). Many later rulings are director rulings under the Owner's delegation (OD-23, OD-34, OD-35), not direct Owner decisions; that is called out per row.
