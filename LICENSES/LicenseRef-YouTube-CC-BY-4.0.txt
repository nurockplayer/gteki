Stands for assets/audio/youtube-cc/*.opus (J11): segments cut from YouTube videos whose
metadata licence field reads, verbatim, "Creative Commons Attribution license (reuse allowed)".
Per-file title, channel, URL, upload date, segment and change note: assets/audio/youtube-cc/SOURCES.md.
The field names no version; this pointer records CC BY 4.0 because that is the licence YouTube's
help page links.

Scope limit: the CC BY declaration covers the video upload only. Departure melodies, door chimes
and recorded announcements inside it can be separate works; segments that may contain them are
marked UNKNOWN-melody / UNKNOWN-voice in SOURCES.md and are audition-only until a separate rights
check. Not relicensed under the repository's grants.
