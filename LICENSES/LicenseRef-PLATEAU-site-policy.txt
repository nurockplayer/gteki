Stands for MLIT Project PLATEAU data and files derived from it (CityGML ubld,
bldg and tran, GLBs, bakes, renders, inventories). The repository's records
label the ubld/bldg/tran data CC BY 4.0 (tools/gis/sources/sources.yaml,
stations/*/gteki/station.json); the PLATEAU data keeps those terms and is not
relicensed under the repository's grants. Road (tran) geometry ships under an
Owner risk acceptance, not a licence finding
(docs/gis/decisions/2026-10-22-plateau-tran-geometry-risk-acceptance.md).
